Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This policy explains what information this website collects, why, and who it is shared with. It covers this website only. Your insurance policy and your dealings with Farmers Insurance are covered by the Farmers Insurance privacy notice, available on farmers.com.

Information you give us

  • Referral form. When you refer someone, we collect their name, phone number and email address, plus your name and your phone number or email. We use this only to contact the person you referred and to let you know how it went.
  • Chat assistant. If you use the chat, the name you enter is used to greet you, and the messages you type are sent to an AI service so it can answer. Please don’t share policy numbers, Social Security numbers, health information or other sensitive details in the chat.
  • Email and phone. If you email or call us, we use what you share to help you.

Information collected automatically

  • Visit statistics. We may use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages are popular. It does not use cookies and does not track you across other websites.
  • Language preference. If you switch the site to Español, your browser remembers that choice on your device. It is not sent to us.
  • Hosting and content providers. Like any website, the services that deliver these pages and their fonts and images (GitHub Pages, Google Fonts, Unsplash) receive your IP address and browser details as part of normal web traffic.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers that run parts of this site, and only for that purpose:

  • Make.com delivers referral form submissions to our office.
  • Cloudflare runs the chat assistant and the visit statistics.
  • OpenAI generates the chat assistant’s replies.
  • Farmers Insurance, when you go on to request a quote or buy a policy.

We may also disclose information if required by law.

Cookies

This website does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Third-party sites we link to, such as Facebook and farmers.com, have their own cookie and privacy policies.

How long we keep it

We keep referral and contact information only as long as needed to follow up, or as required by insurance record-keeping rules.

Your choices

You can ask us to tell you what information we have about you, correct it, or delete it, by contacting us using the details below. If someone referred you and you’d prefer we didn’t contact you, just tell us and we won’t.

Children

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
